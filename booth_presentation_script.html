Evaluator Q&A Cheat Sheet (Top Defense Questions) Memorize these bulletproof answers

Evaluator Question Bulletproof Response (Say This Plainly)
“Why Simple Linear Regression instead of complex Machine Learning like LSTM or Random Forest?” Sample Size & Interpretability: Catering sales follow clean monthly trendlines governed by seasonal contracts. In small-to-medium enterprise catering, SLR achieved an empirical R² score of 0.892 on an 80/20 chronological split. Complex neural networks require thousands of daily data points and risk overfitting on monthly data, while SLR is mathematically transparent and explainable to business owners.
“How do you prevent payment fraud and fake GCash reference numbers?” Server-to-Server Webhook Verification: Clients do not submit manual transfer slips. Payments route through PayMongo API. PayMongo signs every webhook payload with a cryptographic signature header. Our Laravel backend verifies the HMAC signature before marking any invoice as paid, ensuring 100% verified ledger balance.
“What happens if the customer enters an unrealistically low budget in the Maximizer?” Feasibility Constraint Check: The engine calculates the minimum viable 5-course package (&8369;240 × pax × 1.22 taxes/fees). If the budget is below this threshold, the algorithm halts gracefully and outputs the exact shortfall required rather than generating an incomplete menu.
“How does the system benefit the kitchen staff specifically?” Automated Kitchen Prep Sheets: Once a booking is confirmed, ECS aggregates ingredient requirements based on guest headcount and dish recipes, replacing messy handwritten slips with digital, time-stamped culinary prep orders.
“Is this deployed live? Can I test it right now?” Yes! Scan the QR code on our screen or visit https://jdadonato.github.io/ECS-EXHIBIT/. The exhibit is a progressive web app (PWA) with full offline caching and responsive controls for smartphones.
Body Language Key: Stand side-by-side with evaluators rather than behind the table. Let them press the sliders or tap the QR code themselves — hands-on interaction creates the highest evaluation scores!
